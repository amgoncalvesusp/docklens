DockLens 1.0 is a reciprocal-space atlas for auditable molecular-interaction
analysis. The main desktop surface has three peer workspaces: Residues,
Fingerprint and Compare. A compact deep-navy navigation rail and shallow context
bar preserve room for paper-white analytical fields. The reusable DockLens Lens
keeps the selected residue, pose, frame or interval synchronized across plots,
tables and inspectors. Interaction colors follow the Okabe-Ito semantic palette
and never decorate general controls. Docking frequency and molecular-dynamics
occupancy must remain explicitly distinct. The Discovery Studio-like profile is
always visible and its parameters remain auditable. On compact windows,
navigation labels and the inspector collapse before charts lose scientific
width; tables, heatmaps and timelines scroll independently. Every figure must
export with its source rows, filters, counting unit and analysis parameters.
